Our Ingredients

What goes into every Green Crunch bite

🌾 Base & Flours 🧈 Fats & Oils 🍯 Sweeteners 🥣 Leavening 🥛 Liquids 🥜 Inclusions & Aromas
🌾

Core Base & Flours

Structure

Whole Wheat Flour (Atta)

Used for digestive and high-fiber biscuits.

Millet Flours

Ragi (Finger Millet), Bajra (Pearl Millet), Jowar (Sorghum), Foxtail, and Barnyard millet flours.

Oat Flour / Rolled Oats

Adds dietary fiber, texture, and nutty notes.

Cornstarch / Tapioca Starch

Modifies dough strength for an extra-crispy snap.

🧈

Fats & Oils

Texture & Flakiness

Butter

Imparts rich aroma, delicate melt-in-mouth mouthfeel, and golden finish.

Pure Ghee

Traditional fat used for Nankhatai and Indian shortbread biscuits.

Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil

Aromatic plant-based fat for clean-label/vegan lines.

🍯

Sweeteners & Flavor Balancers

Sweetness & Balance

Jaggery Powder / Palm Sugar

Unrefined sweetening agents rich in molasses notes for organic ranges.

Honey / Malt Extract

Aids uniform browning, prevents crystallization, and balances moisture.

Iodized Salt / Sea Salt

Sharpens sweetness and strengthens the gluten network.

🥣

Leavening & Dough Conditioning Agents

Rise & Crunch

Baking Powder

Dual-action chemical leavener for vertical rise and aerated crumb.

Baking Soda (Sodium Bicarbonate)

Aids spread, browning, and light aerated texture.

Soy Lecithin / Plant Lecithin (Emulsifier)

Ensures fat and moisture homogenize uniformly without dough separation.

🥛

Liquids & Hydration

Binding & Richness

Fresh Milk / Skim Milk Powder (SMP)

Adds milky richness, crust color, and binding.

Filtered Water

Hydrates dry starches and dissolves soluble leaveners.

Whole Eggs / Liquid Egg Whites Optional

Adds protein structure, emulsification, and richness (omitted in 100% vegetarian biscuits).

🥜

Inclusions, Spices & Natural Aromas

Flavor & Crunch

Natural Extracts

Real Vanilla Bean Extract, Almond Extract, or Cardamom Oleoresin.

Nuts & Dry Fruits

Roasted Cashews, Almond slivers, Pistachios, and Golden Raisins.

Functional Seeds

Chia seeds, Roasted Flaxseeds, Sesame seeds, and Pumpkin seeds.

Cocoa & Spices

Dutch-processed Cocoa Powder, Cinnamon, Nutmeg, or Ginger powder.
